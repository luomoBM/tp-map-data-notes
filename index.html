Community Data, Clearly Labeled

Source and scope

The map runs on a community dataset (gtadb.org): 3,651 places, 12 categories, unofficial and browser-only. Scope per type: the locations guide.

Approximate by design

Pins are pointers, not extractions from game files. The Vice City page labels its counts with the exact date they were pulled.

Corrections are the QA

Dataset refreshes happen on every page load - send place name + what is wrong via the contact page and it is live next refresh.

Restraint as a feature

The collectibles page ships zero invented spawn points, and the safehouse list stays at four rows instead of padding - counts on the interactive map mean what they say.
